## Star Defender

A small arcade space shooter that runs in the browser. Open `index.html` in any browser to play. You don't need to build or install anything.

- **Move:** ← → or A / D (or drag on a touch screen)
- **Fire:** automatic
- **Pause:** P or Esc
- **Power-ups:** S spread shot · R rapid fire · O shield · + extra life

The game gets harder every 25 seconds, with faster spawns and new enemy types. Your best score is saved in the browser.

## Stack Up

A one-tap stacking game made for phones. Open `stack.html` to play.

- Tap (or press Space) to drop the sliding block onto the tower.
- Any part that hangs over the edge gets cut off, so your blocks get smaller.
- Land it perfectly to keep the full size. Three perfects in a row make the block grow back.
- When you lose, a **Challenge a friend** button shares your score.
