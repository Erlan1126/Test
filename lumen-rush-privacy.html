Lumen Rush privacy policy

Big Bruce Studios LLC · Effective November 26, 2026

Lumen Rush is a game by Big Bruce Studios LLC ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play it. In short: we don't collect anything about you.

What we collect

Nothing. Lumen Rush has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We don't collect your name, email, location, contacts, device identifiers or anything else about you, and we don't sell or share any data.

Your progress stays on your device

Your stages, stars, lights, looks and settings are saved in your browser's local storage on your own device. They never leave it unless you choose to move them yourself with the backup code in Settings. Clearing your browser's site data, or tapping "Erase all progress" in Settings, deletes them.

So the game works offline, your browser also keeps a copy of the game's own files (a service worker cache). It holds only the game, not anything about you.

Sharing and challenge links

If you tap Share, your device's own share sheet opens and you decide where it goes. A shared link carries only game information: the stage number, your time or score, and the path your light took so a friend can race its ghost. It contains nothing that identifies you.

Hosting

The web version of Lumen Rush is served by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) to run and secure its service; see GitHub's privacy statement. The game itself loads nothing from any other site.

Children

Lumen Rush is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no personal information from anyone, we collect none from children.

Changes

If the game ever changes in a way that affects your privacy (for example, an app store version with purchases), we'll update this page and its effective date before that version is released.

Contact

Questions about privacy or anything else: support@bigbrucestudios.com
