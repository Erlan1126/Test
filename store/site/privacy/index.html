Privacy policy

Big Bruce Studios LLC · Last updated November 28, 2026

This policy covers our three games: Buildaroo (the city game), Skyrise (the tower game) and Lumen Rush (the light-swing runner), on the App Store, Google Play and the web. Big Bruce Studios LLC, a Florida company, makes them. Questions: support@bigbrucestudios.com.

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads and no tracking in any of our games.
  • We never sell or share your data, and we don't use it for advertising.
  • Lumen Rush collects nothing about you. Your progress stays on your device, and the only thing it sends is an anonymous crash report if something goes wrong, which you can turn off.
  • Buildaroo and Skyrise send our own server a few things so leaderboards, friends' cities and cloud backup work: a random player id the game makes up, the nickname you choose, your scores and a backup of your progress. They also send anonymous crash reports and play statistics so we can fix bugs and balance the game.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

Buildaroo and Skyrise

The two games share one codebase and one server, so this section applies to both. Where something only happens in one game, it says so.

On your device

Your progress (towers, city, levels, coins, stars, Sky Crystals, skins, settings and a random player id) is saved on your device: in the app's own storage in the phone apps, or in your browser's local storage on the web. A save code you make in Settings contains your progress and is only shared if you copy and paste it yourself. Keep it private, because anyone with it can load your progress and purchases.

The random player id

The first time you play, the game makes up a random player id. It is not your name, email, phone number, or an Apple, Google, device or advertising id. Everything the game sends our server for leaderboards, friends and backup is filed under this id.

Leaderboards

When you finish a ranked tower (Skyrise and the tower mode), the game sends our server your player id, your nickname (the one you choose, or "Builder" plus a few letters of your id), your builder level, and the tower itself: its score, height and the timing of your taps, so the server can replay it and check the score is real. The leaderboard shows your nickname, level and best scores to other players.

Cloud backup

So you don't lose progress or purchases when you change phones, the game backs up your save to our server under your player id: your progress, Sky Crystals, which packs you have bought, skins, settings and nickname. Settings → Cloud backup shows a recovery code; typing it on another device restores the backup there. Keep the code private.

Buildaroo: friends' cities, gifts and the city leaderboard

Every few minutes while you play Buildaroo, the game sends our server a snapshot of your city under a short friend code: your nickname, your city's name, your City Hall level and number of residents, your business levels, homes, decorations and building paints, and how many homes you built this week. Anyone with your friend code or an invite link you share can see that snapshot and visit your city, and the biggest cities appear on the city leaderboard. Friend teams add up the homes each member built this week. A gift you leave in a friend's city is kept, with your nickname, until they collect it, then deleted. There is no chat.

Names and reports

Nicknames and city names go through a word filter. If you report a name, the server records that your player id reported it. Names reported by several players are hidden.

Challenge links

When you tap "Challenge a friend", the tower you share (its height, people, floors, how fast it grew and your nickname, if you set one) is saved on our server so the link can show it. When a friend plays the challenge, their nickname and score are saved with it so you can see who tried.

Crash and performance reports

To fix problems on real phones, the game sends anonymous technical reports: the text of an error if the game crashes, and after each tower how smoothly it ran (frames per second). Each report includes the app version, the phone model and the graphics chip, and nothing else: no player id, nickname, save or location. We keep at most the newest 20,000 reports, for a few weeks.

Anonymous play statistics

To balance the game, it sends anonymous statistics: that the game was opened that day, which steps of the game you reached (for example finishing the first day or opening the shop), and after each tower its mode, floors, height, perfect drops and score, with your level, how many towers this install has built and how many days since it first played. They carry a random id made up for statistics only. It is not your player id and isn't linked to your nickname, save, device or anything else. They're kept in summary form (at most the newest 300,000).

Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone, if you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, the game sends your best tower's people and height to Game Center leaderboards and reports achievements. Apple runs Game Center under Apple's privacy policy; we don't receive your Apple account details.

Reminders and sharing

If you turn on the daily reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone and nothing is sent to us. Share buttons open your phone's own share sheet, and you choose where it goes.

Where it's kept

Our server runs on Cloudflare (United States and worldwide), which handles the network requests to it. All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS).

Lumen Rush

Lumen Rush collects nothing about you. It has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your stages, stars, lights, looks and settings are saved on your device and never leave it unless you move them yourself with the backup code in Settings. "Erase all progress" in Settings, or clearing the site's data, deletes them.

Crash reports. If the game hits an error, it sends an anonymous crash report to our own report server so we can fix it. A report holds only the error message and where in the game's code it happened, the game version, the phone or browser model and screen size, and what the game was doing (for example "run · stage 12"). It holds no name, account, player id, progress or contact details, and nothing that links reports to you or to each other. Only the most recent reports are kept, and they are used only to fix bugs. Nothing is sent when nothing goes wrong. To stop them, turn off "Send crash reports" in Settings.

A Share link carries only game information (the stage, your time or score, and the path your light took so a friend can race its ghost), and you choose where it goes. The web version is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses; see GitHub's privacy statement. The game loads nothing from any other site; the only thing it sends is a crash report, described above.

If a future version adds anything that changes this (for example purchases in a store version), we'll update this section before that version is released.

Purchases

Buildaroo and Skyrise are free, with optional purchases of Sky Crystals, a Starter Pack and a VIP monthly subscription. Lumen Rush has no purchases.

  • In the phone apps, Apple or Google handles the payment and your account details. The game only learns which product was bought, so it can add it. Restore purchases in Settings asks the store again. Their policies apply: Apple, Google.
  • On the website, Stripe handles payment on its own checkout page; the game never sees your card. Stripe may ask for your email for the receipt (Stripe's privacy policy). Our server stores only your player id and the packs you bought, with Stripe's order number.

Children

All three games are made for everyone. We don't ask anyone for a real name, email, phone number, location, contacts or photos. In Buildaroo and Skyrise the only text a player types that others can see is the optional nickname (and in Buildaroo the city's name), which is filtered and can be reported. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13; if you believe a child has entered personal information as a nickname or city name, email us and we'll delete it.

Deleting your data and your rights

Email support@bigbrucestudios.com with your nickname, friend code or recovery code (Settings → Cloud backup) and tell us what you'd like: a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion of your leaderboard entries, city snapshot and backup. We answer within 30 days. Anonymous reports and statistics can't be linked back to you, so they can't be looked up or deleted individually; they expire on their own. Wherever you live, including the EU, UK and California, you have these rights, and we never sell or share personal information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for significant changes, mention it in the games' update notes.
