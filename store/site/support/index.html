Support

Something not working, a purchase that didn't arrive, or a question? Email support@bigbrucestudios.com. A real person answers, usually within two business days.

It helps to include the game's name, your phone model and whether you play in the app or a browser (for example "Buildaroo, iPhone 15, app"), what happened, and for Buildaroo and Skyrise your nickname or friend code.

A purchase didn't arrive

  1. Close the game completely and open it again, so it can check with the store.
  2. Open Settings → Restore purchases. This brings back the VIP subscription and anything you own, for example on a new phone.
  3. Still missing? Email us with the store's receipt (the order number starting with GPA. on Google Play, or the order ID on Apple's receipt email) and we'll sort it out.

Refunds

Refunds for purchases in the phone apps are handled by the store you paid:

  • iPhone: go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose "Request a refund".
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history, or use Google's refund request form.
  • Website purchases (Stripe): email us within 14 days with the email on your receipt and we'll refund the full price. It usually shows on your statement within 5 to 10 business days.

VIP subscription

VIP renews every month until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge, and you keep VIP until the end of the period you paid for.

  • iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Buildaroo VIP or Skyrise VIP → Cancel Subscription.
  • Android: Google Play → your profile picture → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → the game → Cancel subscription.

Deleting the game does not cancel the subscription.

Moving your progress to a new phone

Buildaroo and Skyrise: on your old phone, open Settings → Cloud backup and write down the recovery code. On the new phone, install the game, tap Played before? Restore your progress (or go to Settings → Cloud backup) and type the code. Your purchases come with it. Keep the code private: anyone who has it can load your progress.

Lumen Rush: open Settings → Backup code, copy the code, and paste it into the same place on the new device.

Deleting your data

Lumen Rush keeps nothing on our side; deleting the game or using Settings → Erase all progress removes everything.

For Buildaroo and Skyrise, email support@bigbrucestudios.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your nickname, friend code or recovery code. We delete your leaderboard entries, city snapshot, challenge links and cloud backup within 30 days and reply to confirm. Anonymous crash reports and statistics aren't linked to you and expire on their own. See the privacy policy for details.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. None of our games has accounts or sign-in.

Do the games work offline?

Yes. Buildaroo and Skyrise need a connection only for leaderboards, friends' cities, cloud backup and purchases; Lumen Rush never needs one after it's installed.

Is anything I can buy random?

No. Every paid item shows exactly what you get before you buy it.

Someone's nickname is rude.

Tap the name on the leaderboard and choose Report. Names reported by several players are hidden. You can also email us.

The game runs slowly on my phone.

Turn on Low graphics or Reduce motion in Settings if your game has it, close other apps, and make sure the phone isn't in low-power mode. If it's still slow, email us your phone model; the game also sends us an anonymous frame-rate report so we can find which phones need work.
