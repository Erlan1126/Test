# Skyrise: the main purpose (Claude + ChatGPT, combined)

Two reviews, done separately, came to the same answer:
- ChatGPT's code walkthrough: `docs/CHATGPT-PURPOSE.md` (branch `chatgpt/skyrise-purpose-review`).
- Claude's playthrough from a fresh save (first tower, the neighbor, the café, a simulated day 2).

## The purpose, in one sentence

**Grow Skyhaven from one neighbor into the Capital of the Sky.**

Towers bring people and office space. People bring business. Business grows the town, rank by
rank (Hamlet → Village → Town → City → Big City → Metropolis → Sky Capital). Every rank is
something new you can see, and the neighbor's story goes on with it. Money is the means, not
the goal.

## What both of us saw

- The game has a real beginning (the first neighbor moves in), middle (neighborhoods grow) and end
  (Sky Capital), but it never says so. The screen shows money and short tasks.
- Too many progress systems compete: builder level, Mayor Pip chapters, town rank, daily goals,
  missions, Sky Pass. A new player sees a "Level up! Apprentice" screen after their first tower
  and "Chapter 1: build 3 towers" on day 2. Neither is about their town.
- The neighbor, the café terrace, the flower market and the playground are the best thing in the
  game. They should get more attention, not less.

## Done in this build

1. **The goal is always on screen.** A bar in the town: "Goal: grow Skyhaven to the Sky Capital,
   Village → Town 120/150". Tap it to see Pip's whole plan: every rank, what it adds to the town, and
   how far you are.
2. **Every tower says what it did for the town:** "+16 office floors · 34 business levels until
   Village".
3. **Coming back leads with the town:** the welcome-back note says where Skyhaven stands and what the
   next rank brings, next to the money earned.
4. **The neighbor's next chapter:** once the flower market is open, the neighbor gets up from their
   terrace table now and then, walks to the market, buys a bouquet and carries it back. A vase of
   flowers stays on their table for good.
5. **Tram stops (Town rank):** two shelters on the tram's route. The tram slows in and waits;
   people waiting at the stop get on, two passengers get off and walk into town, and the stop fills
   up again before it comes back.
6. Earlier in this round: shoppers carry what each shop sells, the rank stays through a New Era, and
   every rank adds a visible landmark.

## Next, in order (not built yet)

1. **One progress system in front.** Make Mayor Pip's chapters the story of the ranks (chapter n =
   rank n) and move builder levels, missions and the Sky Pass out of the first session.
2. **A neighbor story beat for every rank:** at Town the neighbor takes the tram; at City they open
   a shop of their own; at Sky Capital they're at the golden tower's opening.
3. **Camera visits each rank's new landmark** the moment the rank is reached.
4. **A City Plan "next step":** the Next step card always picks the action that brings the next
   visible change first, before generic upgrades.
5. **Test with five new players:** "What is the goal? What did your tower change? What are you
   looking forward to?" If they can't answer, fix the words before adding features.
