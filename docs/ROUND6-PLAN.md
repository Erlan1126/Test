# Round 6: what Claude and ChatGPT agreed on, and what's in this build

ChatGPT's full answer: `docs/CHATGPT-ROUND6.md`. Claude's ideas from round 5: a guided first loop,
"Why?" on locked buttons, units, scrapbook pictures, better graphics.

**What makes Skyrise different (both agree):** the tower you build by hand changes a small town
that you can see, and your first neighbor notices it. *"Look what your tower made possible."*

## In this build
- **Skyhaven Remembers** (ChatGPT's signature idea, first part): a real picture of the town is
  taken the first time you see it and at every new rank; the scrapbook shows them, and a
  **Then → Now** card (your first picture beside today's town) can be shared with a friend.
- **A note from your neighbor** (ChatGPT's daily hook #1): once a day the neighbor says one line
  about what changed since your last visit, or just says hello. No guilt, no fake emergencies.
- **First day in Skyhaven** (Claude): five steps with dots on the Next step card.
- **"Why?"** (Claude): tap a greyed-out button and it says what it needs and how long that takes.
- **Units**: the rank badge shows business levels; the guide explains K/M/B/T.
- **A bigger island**: a countryside ring with farm fields, sheep, a windmill and a winding
  country lane; flower roundabouts where the town's streets cross.
- **Better people and buildings**: people have legs, arms, hair and hats (one draw each);
  buildings have window frames, ledges, cornices and rooftop details.
- **New top bar** (a round level badge with a progress ring, money, crystals, help) and **new
  bottom dock** (colored tiles, a big round Build button).
- **Less lag**: the town's shadows are redrawn only when the view moves.
