Build the tallest skyscraper in the world, one tap at a time.

A floor slides back and forth above your tower. Tap to drop it. Whatever hangs over
the edge breaks off, so every miss makes your tower a little narrower. Line it up
perfectly and the floor keeps its full width, and wider floors fit more people.
How many people can you house before you run out of room?

## How to play

1. Wait for the sliding floor to line up with the one below.
2. Tap, click or press Space to drop it.
3. Keep stacking. Perfect drops keep your tower wide. Miss completely and the tower is done.

## What you'll find

- Real landmarks to beat: climb past a house, Niagara Falls (51 m), the Statue of Liberty (93 m), the Great Pyramid (139 m), the Space Needle (184 m), the Golden Gate Bridge (227 m), the Eiffel Tower (330 m), the Empire State Building (443 m) and the Burj Khalifa (828 m). The next one shows up as a silhouette beside your tower.
- Special floors: gold penthouses pay 5x on a perfect drop, icy floors slide fast but hold more people, and garden terraces slow down and widen your tower again.
- Modes: Classic, a Daily tower that's the same for everyone each day, relaxing Zen mode with no way to fail, and six Landmark challenges with their own rules.
- Something new every day: three missions and a daily chest, a play streak with freezes for days off, rent from your tower, and a weekly league from Bronze to Legend.
- Your own floating island: houses, a farm, a lake and a waterfall, plus a city you build up with coins (café, windmill, lighthouse, rocket pad and more).
- A sky that changes as you climb: day turns to sunset and night, with lit windows, fireflies, the aurora, rain, snow and lightning, and planes, airships and even a UFO at altitude.
- 19 tower skins, island themes, trophies, builder levels and a star shop with boosts.
- Calm synthesized music and ambience that change with your height and the time of day.

## Controls

- Drop a floor: tap, click, or press Space
- Pause: Esc or P

## Premium is cosmetic only

Skyrise is free to play. There are a few optional premium items (4 tower skins,
2 islands and a Founder's Pack) that change how your tower and island look.
They never help you score, and everything else is unlocked by playing.
Payments go through Stripe. Skyrise never sees your card details.

## Good to know

- Free, no download, no account. Works on phones, tablets and desktop browsers.
- Your progress is saved in your browser on this device. To play on another device,
  open Settings → Move progress to another device and copy your save code.
- Privacy: see the privacy note below the game.
