# Skyrise play-test checklist

Play on a real phone for a few days, like a normal player would. When something feels
off, write a quick note: **what you did, what happened, what you expected**, plus a
screenshot or screen recording if you can. Send the notes to Claude in batches.

## Day 1: first impressions (do this before you know the game too well)

To start fresh: uninstall, reinstall, and **don't** restore your progress.

- [ ] Did the first tower make sense without anyone explaining it?
- [ ] Was any screen confusing, or did you not know what to tap next?
- [ ] Did new features (missions, city, Sky Pass, shop) appear at a good pace, or too many at once?
- [ ] Was any text cut off, overlapping, or hard to read?
- [ ] Rate from 1 to 10: how much did you want to play "one more tower"?

## Feel and performance

- [ ] Does the floor move smoothly, with no stutters, especially on tall towers and at night with rain or snow?
- [ ] Do taps register instantly? Does a drop ever feel unfair ("I tapped on time!")?
- [ ] Is the speed at higher floors fun or frustrating?
- [ ] Sound: is it too loud, too quiet or annoying? Does the music fit?
- [ ] Vibration: does it feel good, or is it too much?
- [ ] Does the phone get hot, or the battery drain fast?
- [ ] Graphics: try Settings → Graphics → Smooth. Does it look much worse, or run much better?

## Phone behavior

- [ ] Android back button: closes screens, pauses during play, and leaves the app from the menu?
- [ ] Leave the app mid-tower (home button or a phone call), then come back: is it paused, with nothing lost?
- [ ] Turn on the daily reminder (🔥 streak screen). Does the notification arrive at the right time, and not on days you already played?
- [ ] Share a tower from the game-over screen: does the share sheet and picture work?
- [ ] Turn on airplane mode and play a Classic tower. Turn it off and reopen: did the tower reach the rankings?
- [ ] Rotate the phone: does anything look broken?

## Progress and money

- [ ] Write down your recovery code (Settings → Cloud backup). Later, reinstall and restore with it: is everything back?
- [ ] Do missions, the Sky Pass and level-ups feel rewarding, or slow and grindy?
- [ ] Do coins, stars and crystals make sense, or are three currencies confusing?
- [ ] Are crystal prices and the Sky Pass price (500 💎) fair? Would you buy anything?
- [ ] After Google Play setup: buy a pack as a **license tester** (free). Did the crystals arrive once, and only once?

## Online

- [ ] Do your scores show in Ranks (today, this week, all time)?
- [ ] Does your nickname save, and does it show correctly?
- [ ] Does the rival line on the menu make sense?

## Language

- [ ] Switch to Español (Settings → Idioma). Does anything sound unnatural, or is any text cut off?

## Quick notes template

```
Where: (screen or moment)
What I did:

What I expected:

```
