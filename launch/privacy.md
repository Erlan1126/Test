# Skyrise privacy policy

Skyrise is made by Erlan Studios (contact: erlanstudios1@gmail.com).
Last updated: November 4, 2026 (worldwide leaderboard and Sky Crystals added).

Short version: Skyrise has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on
your device.

## What the game stores

- **Your progress is stored on your device**, in your browser's local storage (in the
  phone apps, in the app's own storage, with a backup copy kept by the app): your
  towers, levels, coins, stars, skins, settings and a random player id. It is not sent to
  us. Clearing your browser's site data deletes it.
- **Save codes** (Settings → Move progress to another device) are created on your device.
  They contain your progress. They are only shared if you copy and paste them yourself.
  Keep your save code private, since anyone with it can load your progress and purchases.

## The worldwide leaderboard

When you finish a Classic or Daily tower, the game sends it to the Skyrise server so it
can appear on the worldwide leaderboard:

- a **random player id** the game made for you (not your name, email, or a device or
  advertising id),
- the **nickname** you choose in the leaderboard screen (or "Builder" plus a few letters
  of your id if you don't choose one) and your builder level,
- the tower itself: its **score, height, and the timing of your taps**, so the server can
  replay it and make sure the score is real.

The leaderboard shows your nickname, level and best scores to other players. The server
runs on Cloudflare. We don't sell or share this data, and it isn't used for ads or
tracking. To have your leaderboard entries deleted, email erlanstudios1@gmail.com with
your nickname.

## What the game does not do

- No accounts or sign-in (the leaderboard uses the random player id above).
- No ads.
- No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party trackers.

## Crystal purchases on the website

Sky Crystals and everything they buy are optional and cosmetic. If you buy a pack on the website:

- **Stripe handles your payment.** You pay on Stripe's checkout page. Stripe handles your
  card details, and the game never sees them. Stripe may ask for your email address for
  the receipt. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to that: https://stripe.com/privacy
- **Our server stores only your random player id and the packs you bought** (for example
  `crystals_550` with Stripe's order number). That is how the game knows to add them. It
  does not store your name, email or card details. The server runs on Cloudflare, which
  handles the network requests to it.

## Purchases in the phone apps

In the iPhone and Android apps, Sky Crystal packs are bought through the **App Store** or
**Google Play**. Apple or Google handles the payment and your account details; the game
only learns which pack was bought, so it can add the crystals. Their privacy policies apply:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Reminders

The phone apps can show a daily reminder notification if you turn it on in the streak
screen. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. Turn it off there or in your
phone's settings at any time.

## Fonts

The game's fonts are included with the game, so it doesn't contact any font service.

## Children

Skyrise is suitable for all ages and doesn't collect personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Where the game is hosted

When you play on itch.io, itch.io's own privacy policy applies to its website:
https://itch.io/docs/legal/privacy-policy

## Contact

Questions about privacy, or want your purchase record deleted? Contact
