# Skyrise privacy policy

Skyrise is made by Erlan Studios (contact: erlanstudios1@gmail.com).
Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Short version: Skyrise has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress is saved on
your device and backed up to our server under a random player id, so you can get it back
on a new phone. We never sell or share it.

## What the game stores

- **Your progress is stored on your device**, in your browser's local storage (in the
  phone apps, in the app's own storage, with a backup copy kept by the app): your
  towers, levels, coins, stars, skins, settings and a random player id. A copy is also
  kept on our server (see "Cloud backup" below).
- **Save codes** (Settings → Move progress to another device) are created on your device.
  They contain your progress. They are only shared if you copy and paste them yourself.
  Keep your save code private, since anyone with it can load your progress and purchases.

## The worldwide leaderboard

When you finish a Classic or Daily tower, the game sends it to the Skyrise server so it
can appear on the worldwide leaderboard:

- a **random player id** the game made for you (not your name, email, or a device or
  advertising id),
- the **nickname** you choose in the leaderboard screen (or "Builder" plus a few letters
  of your id if you don't choose one) and your builder level,
- the tower itself: its **score, height, and the timing of your taps**, so the server can
  replay it and make sure the score is real.

The leaderboard shows your nickname, level and best scores to other players. The server
runs on Cloudflare. We don't sell or share this data, and it isn't used for ads or
tracking. To have your leaderboard entries deleted, email erlanstudios1@gmail.com with
your nickname.

## Cloud backup

So you don't lose your progress or purchases when you change phones or reinstall, the
game backs up your save to the Skyrise server: your towers, levels, coins, stars, Sky
Crystals, skins, settings and nickname, linked to your random player id. Settings →
Cloud backup shows a **recovery code**; typing it on another device restores the backup
there. Keep the code private, since anyone with it can restore your progress. To have
your backup deleted, email erlanstudios1@gmail.com with your recovery code.

## Reporting names

If you report a leaderboard name, the server records that you reported it (by random
player id). Names reported by several players are hidden.

## Crash and performance reports

To find and fix problems on real phones, the game sends anonymous technical reports to the
Skyrise server: the text of an error if the game crashes, and after each tower how smoothly
it ran (frames per second). Each report includes the app version, the phone model and the
graphics chip, and nothing else: no player id, nickname, save or location. Reports are kept
for at most a few weeks (the newest 20,000) and are never shared or sold.

## Anonymous play statistics

To balance the game (how hard towers are, how fast rewards come), it sends anonymous play
statistics to the Skyrise server: once a day that the game was opened, and after each tower
its mode, floors, height, perfect drops, score and your builder level, with how many towers
this install has built and how many days since it first played. They carry a random id made
up by this install, which is not your player id and isn't linked to your nickname, save,
device or anything else, plus the app version and the game's language. They're kept in
summary form (the newest 300,000) and are never shared or sold.

## Challenge links

When you tap "Challenge a friend", the tower you share (its height, people, floors, how
fast it grew, and your leaderboard nickname if you set one) is saved on the Skyrise server
so the link can show it. When a friend plays the challenge, their nickname and score are
saved with it so you can see who tried.

## What the game does not do

- No accounts or sign-in (the leaderboard uses the random player id above).
- No ads.
- No analytics, tracking pixels or third-party trackers.

## Crystal purchases on the website

Sky Crystals and everything they buy are optional and cosmetic. If you buy a pack on the website:

- **Stripe handles your payment.** You pay on Stripe's checkout page. Stripe handles your
  card details, and the game never sees them. Stripe may ask for your email address for
  the receipt. Stripe's own privacy policy applies to that: https://stripe.com/privacy
- **Our server stores only your random player id and the packs you bought** (for example
  `crystals_650` with Stripe's order number). That is how the game knows to add them. It
  does not store your name, email or card details. The server runs on Cloudflare, which
  handles the network requests to it.

## Purchases in the phone apps

In the iPhone and Android apps, Sky Crystal packs are bought through the **App Store** or
**Google Play**. Apple or Google handles the payment and your account details; the game
only learns which pack was bought, so it can add the crystals. Their privacy policies apply:
https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/ and https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Reminders

The phone apps can show a daily reminder notification if you turn it on in the streak
screen. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. Turn it off there or in your
phone's settings at any time.

## Fonts

The game's fonts are included with the game, so it doesn't contact any font service.

## Children

Skyrise is suitable for all ages. It doesn't ask for names, emails, phone numbers,
locations or photos from anyone. The only thing a player types that others can see is the
optional leaderboard nickname, and players can report names that aren't appropriate.

## Contact

Questions about privacy, or want your data deleted? Contact erlanstudios1@gmail.com
and include your nickname or recovery code.
